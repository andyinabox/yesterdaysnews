The videos you are seeing are a random reshuffling of news clips from the day before today. The text is generated from the captions for those videos, alongside a journal of mine from late October to early November 2016. The videos are purely random, but the text follows its own internal logic. 

Every day a new cycle, and we're meant to believe that _yesterday's news_ is no longer relevant. But every day leaves its mark, is inexorably linked to the one before it.

You can read more about this project and access the source code [here](https://gitlab.com/andyinabox/yesterdaysnews/-/blob/main/README.md).
