The videos you are seeing are a random reshuffling of news clips from the day before today. The text is generated from the captions of those news clips, alongside a transcribed journal from November of 2016. The videos are purely random, but the text follows its own internal logic. 

Every day a new cycle, and we are meant to believe that yesterday's news is no longer relevant. But every day leaves its mark, and finds its way back into the story as we continue on. Every day follows another, and is inextricably linked to the one that came before it.

You can see the source code for this project [here](https://gitlab.com/andyinabox/yesterdaysnews).
